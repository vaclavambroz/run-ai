# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all
contributors regardless of background or experience level.

## Our standards

Expected behaviour:
- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Accept feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users

Unacceptable behaviour:
- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to vaclav@ambroz.email.
All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with confidentiality.
